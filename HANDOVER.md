## Goal
A visually impressive demo link to win the client's confidence. It must work on phones first, then tablets and desktop. The tutor and its marking now run on the real syllabus; the scanner and English marking call n8n.

## Decisions made
- **Visual direction:** an illustrated game world in a warm picture-book style (world map, quests, gems, streaks), with real photos as postcards and banners.
- **Imagery:** Unsplash photos for every world, plus client-licensed photos of real wrestlers and footballers in the Wrestling and Football worlds (see Photos).
- **Buddy:** preset cartoon characters. The preview has one buddy, Nova the fox.
- **Child avatar:** built from parts by default; photo-to-cartoon via n8n and gpt-image-2 behind parent consent, photo not stored. Approved by the team.
- **Worlds:** the child picks 1 to 6 worlds; only picked worlds appear.
- **Every world covers the whole syllabus** for the child's year (maths and English). Worlds differ in wording, characters and story, and maths numbers differ per world, so a child who finishes Wrestling gets fresh questions in Football.
- **Progress is separate per world** (team decision, 10 Nov).
- **Stages (10 Nov):** every world has an 8-stage ladder shown on Home and on the world screen; a stage unlocks as the child answers more of that world's questions (2%, 6%, 12%, 22%, 36%, 55%, 80%), with a "New stage unlocked" card in the tutor. Wrestling: Opening match → Main-event match → US Title → Tag Team Title → Intercontinental Title → UK Title → Universal Title → WWE Title. Football: Academy debut → First goal → Starting XI → Top-four push → Golden Boot race → League champions → Player of the Season → Ballon d'Or. Music, Story Forest, Brick City and Space have their own ladders. All names live in `src/data/stages.ts`.
- **Football season (10 Nov):** the child picks a favourite Premier League club (2026–27 clubs, names only, no badges). Every right answer = a win and 3 points; right first time = a goal, right after a hint or a wrong try = an assist; answer shown = a loss. Home and the Football world show league position, points, goals and assists; the world screen shows the full 20-club table, the Golden Boot chart and the assists chart. Rival results and rival players are invented (no real players).
- **Photos (10 Nov):** 11 photos per world; each question card shows the next photo in the set (each quest starts at a different one), with the photographer credited on the photo.
- **World framing:** Football = league season (quests are matches, 3 points a win, live league table); Wrestling = title run (rankings to the Thunder Belt); Music = world tour (cities, songs, fans); Story Forest = The Lost Map (chapters, map pieces); Brick City = city build (districts, floors); Space = mission to the stars (planets, fuel cells, km).
- **Questions are hard-coded, not generated by AI** (cost and reliability). Maths (Hamza's bank) and English (Claude's bank) are marked in code. AI is used only for: English open answers (writing, explain-your-answer), English typed answers that match nothing in the accepted list, and the homework scanner.
- **RAG is for the homework scanner only:** n8n retrieves the selected year's curriculum statements (maths and/or English) and gives them to the vision model, which marks the sheet and tags each question with a curriculum ID. The curriculum is small (about 60–70 statements per year), so retrieval is by year and subject rather than a vector store. Move to vectors only if long syllabus documents are added later.
- **Years:** 4, 5 and 6 (matches the maths bank). Year 7 is deferred until there is a KS3 bank.
- **Curriculum version:** the 2014 National Curriculum for England. A revised curriculum is due to be published in 2027 for first teaching from September 2028.
- **Star names:** football and wrestling use the star cast in `src/data/stars.ts` (invented names). Changing a star there updates maths and English questions and the reading passages. Check real names with the client first (many ring names are WWE trademarks).
- **Tool principle:** quality first, not open source first.
- **Deployment:** Netlify from GitHub.

## What's built
- **Passcode screen**, **child intake** (name, year 4–6, worlds, avatar) and **app shell** (tab bar on phones, side rail at 1024px+), as before.
- **Home:** greeting, streak, gems and level (from real progress), "Today's quest" (next maths quest in the first world), **"Your journeys"**: one card per picked world with its photo, current stage, a stage road (done / current / locked), "N more answers to reach [next stage]" and Play; the Football card adds team, position, points, goals, assists and "Pick your Premier League team". Then Nova tip, daily missions, Maths and English progress. (The illustrated world map is no longer shown on Home; the component is still in `art.tsx`.)
- **World screen** (tap a world): banner, journey pitch, "Continue / Up next", Maths | English tabs with % done, every topic of the year (named per world: Matchweek 1, The Moon, London…) with every quest, progress, stars, Play / Continue / Replay. Football also shows the league table. Maths statements not built yet (diagram and interactive ones) show "Coming soon".
- **Tutor (rebuilt):** plays any quest in any world. Chat with Nova; question card with the world photo, the world's lead-in ("24th minute", "Round 3", "T-minus 9"), level dots and a star card for football and wrestling; 3-level hint ladder; "Show me the answer" after 3 hints or 2 misses; specific mistake messages; confetti and gems; resume where you left off; quest-complete card with stars and "Next match / bout / song…".
  - Maths: number box with a +/− key; marked by the bank's `checkAnswer` (accepts 7.5, 7.50, £7.50, 1,250, −2).
  - English: tap-to-choose answers; spelling (Nova reads the word, the sentence, then the word, using the browser's British voice; "So close!" for one-letter slips); short typed answers (punctuation checked where it matters); open answers sent to AI marking. If n8n can't be reached, Nova shows the example answer and moves on.
- **Homework scanner (Scan tab):** pick Maths, English or "Not sure"; take a photo or choose one; preview; scanning animation; results with score, praise, each question (child's answer, right answer, feedback, steps, curriculum statement) and "Practise this in [world]", which opens the matching quest. "Try a sample sheet" shows a labelled sample for the child's year (works without n8n).
- **Read and Grown-ups tabs:** placeholder screens.

## Question banks
| | Year 4 | Year 5 | Year 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maths statements (built / total) | 30 / 42 | 40 / 49 | 42 / 49 |
| Maths questions per world | 378 | 462 | 482 |
| English statements | 20 | 22 | 20 |
| English items per world | 201 | 161 | 150 |

- **Maths:** Hamza's bank in `src/data/bank/` (205 templates and hand-written items; 7,932 rendered questions pass `verify`). Each world uses its own slice of seeds, so numbers differ per world (hand-written items keep fixed numbers by design). 28 statements (23 DIAGRAM, 5 INTERACTIVE) are not built yet; see the tagging workbook.
- **English:** `src/data/english/`. Spelling (Appendix 1 rules and the statutory word lists: Years 3–4 list in Year 4, Years 5–6 list split across Years 5 and 6), grammar and punctuation (Appendix 2), reading comprehension (one original passage per world, own questions per year: retrieval, inference, vocabulary, prediction/summary, fact and opinion or the writer's choices) and writing (open, AI-marked). About 85% of items are marked in code. Word lists are split into quests of 10 words.
- **Checks:** `npm run verify` runs both bank verifiers (broken text, spacing, answers in options, right answers accepted, wrong options rejected, wording differs by world). A separate run checked all 11,004 quest questions in all 6 worlds through the tutor's real marking path: 0 problems.

## n8n workflows (to import)
Files in `n8n/`, generated by `npm run n8n` from the app's curriculum (rebuild after curriculum changes rather than editing by hand). Import each in n8n (Workflows → Import from file), open the OpenAI node and select the credential **"OpenAi c2 account"**, then publish.

| Workflow | Webhook | In (form fields) | Out (JSON) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Study Buddy: Homework Scanner | `POST /webhook/study-buddy-homework` | `image` (JPEG base64), `year` (4–6), `subject` (maths, english, auto) | `subject, readable, summary, score {correct,total}, items[] {question, child_answer, status, correct_answer, feedback, steps[], curriculum_id}, praise, next_step` |
| Study Buddy: English Marking | `POST /webhook/study-buddy-mark` | `year, curriculum_id, statement, task, extract, rubric, model_answer, answer` | `verdict (correct, almost, not_yet), feedback, tip` |

- Flow: Webhook → Code (validate, retrieve the year's curriculum, build the request) → valid? → OpenAI chat completions with a strict JSON schema → Code (parse; scanner recounts the score) → Respond. Errors return `{ error }` with 400, 413 or 502, which the app shows kindly.
- Model: `gpt-5-mini` with low reasoning, set in the first Code node (`MODEL`). For a non-reasoning model the node drops `reasoning_effort` automatically.
- Privacy: execution saving is off, so photos and answers are not kept. Prompts tell the model not to repeat names from the sheet and to ignore instructions written on the sheet or in answers.
- App URLs default to the Cleartwo instance; override with `VITE_SCAN_WEBHOOK_URL` and `VITE_MARK_WEBHOOK_URL`.
- Not yet tested against live OpenAI from here (the Code nodes were run locally with sample inputs and replies). Test with a real sheet after import.

## Risks to fix before wider sharing
- **Public webhooks:** the avatar, scanner and marking webhooks are public URLs in a public repo, and each call costs OpenAI credit. Make the repo private, and/or add a shared-secret header check plus a rate limit in n8n.
- **Passcode** is visible in the public repo; it only keeps casual visitors out.
- **Children's photos and answers** go to OpenAI (with consent for the avatar). Before a real launch: UK GDPR and ICO Children's Code review, DPIA, parental consent flow for the scanner.
- **Wrestling photo** is client-supplied and shows real WWE wrestlers and branding; usage rights are the client's to confirm.
- **Real names as text:** the wrestling stage names (US, Intercontinental, Universal, WWE titles), "Ballon d'Or" and the Premier League club names are trademarks used as plain text with no logos. Fine for a private demo; confirm with the client (and consider generic names) before a public launch. Everything is editable in `src/data/stages.ts`.
- **Photos load from Unsplash** (hotlinked, free licence, credited on each photo). Chosen from descriptions without people close up or logos; give them a quick visual check on the live site and swap any that look off in `src/data/photos.ts`.
- **Bundle size** is about 250 KB gzipped (the banks ship with the app). Fine for the demo; split per year later if needed.

## Updating the live site
Unzip into the local repo folder (when replacing the whole app, delete the old `src` first), then in PowerShell:
```
$env:Path += ";C:\Program Files\Git\cmd;$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\Git\cmd"
cd "C:\Users\HamzaAbid\OneDrive - Cleartwo LTD\Desktop\New folder (2)"
npm install
git add -A
git commit -m "Describe the change"
git push
```
Netlify redeploys automatically within a minute or two. Run `npm install` once after this update (adds `tsx`).
