# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind 3, chosen to match Lovable's hosting stack. Coded directly, deployed on Lovable for a live demo URL. Client demo only; the production build is planned separately in Flutter.

## Users

- Primary: children aged roughly 7-12 (UK Year 4-6 for this demo), practising Maths on a tablet at home, often after school.
- Secondary: a parent who sets up the child's profile on a phone or tablet and checks what was practised and where the gaps are.
- Demo audience: the client and investors evaluating the concept against Atom Learning, Century, IXL, Seneca and Sparx.

## Product Purpose

Study Buddy turns curriculum-aligned Maths practice into short, adaptive, game-like sessions built around what the child already loves (football, wrestling, K-pop, Lego, music and similar). Success for the demo: one complete journey - parent setup, interests, cartoon avatar, a themed quiz with step-by-step hints, and a parent progress view - that feels clearly better than the competitors.

## Positioning

Personalisation by interest, not just by level: the same Year 5 skill is reframed around the child's world, with a friendly AI buddy that gives hints instead of answers. Every answer is checked by deterministic code, never by AI judgement.

## Operating Context

- Child uses a tablet browser, landscape or portrait, short sessions (5-10 minutes).
- Parent sets up once, then glances at progress.
- Content maps to the UK National Curriculum (England), Year 4-6 Maths.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Demo scope: Maths only, Year 4-6, preset avatars only (no photos), synthetic data only, no real child data stored.
- Questions and hints are pre-written for the demo; answers are verified in code.
- Out of scope: homework scanner, reading coach/voice, English and Science, payments, real accounts.
- Concept is under NDA: the demo sits behind a simple passcode screen.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Study Buddy. Tagline from the concept: "make screen time good".
- Child-first and supportive; never shaming wrong answers.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, customer numbers, school partnerships, results data or certifications exist. Do not fabricate any. Progress data in the demo is the child's own session data only.

## Product Principles

1. The child's interests lead; the curriculum sits underneath.
2. Hints before answers: guide the child to the solution.
3. Correctness is non-negotiable and checked by rules, not AI.
4. Parents see clear, honest progress, not vanity metrics.
5. Calm, safe and ad-free: no dark patterns, no endless streak pressure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readers aged 7-12, including children with ADHD and other learning differences: large touch targets, plain language, minimal text per screen, no time pressure by default, reduced motion respected, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
