## Direction contract

THESIS: Practice is a build. Each question is a numbered build step and every correct answer clicks a row of bricks into a model the child chose. Refuses the category default of a points bar, star counter and mascot on a white card.

OWN-WORLD: Instruction-booklet sky blue ground (#CFE3F6), white step panels, navy ink (#14284B), four brick colours as roles: red #D9312B (wrong/attention, sparingly), yellow #F6C21C (hint, parts callout), blue #1C5FCC (primary action, selection), green #2E9E4F (correct). Bricks drawn as rectangles with top studs. Big black step numerals. Parts-callout boxes with a light blue fill and "2x" counts.

STORY: Child sees what they are building, understands each step gets them closer, uses hints as a parts list instead of giving up, finishes with a complete model. Parent sees honest skill-by-skill progress.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Quiz screen, tablet landscape: left two thirds a white step panel with a huge step numeral top-left, themed question, numeric answer pad; right third the model under construction on the blue ground, completed rows solid, next row ghosted. Primary action "Check answer" bottom-right of the panel. Hint ladder opens inline as a yellow parts callout.
